1. Executive Summary & Project Motivation

Sign Connect AI is an advanced, real-time, bidirectional assistive telecommunication platform engineered to bridge the communication barrier between deaf or hard-of-hearing sign language users and non-signing individuals during live video calls. While standard video conferencing tools (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet) provide passive video streams, they lack native comprehension of sign language gestures. Sign Connect AI integrates computer vision, deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs), natural language processing (NLP), speech synthesis, and WebRTC streaming directly into a zero-install browser environment.

Core Value Proposition
A single, seamless interface where a user can make signs in front of their webcam, have those signs classified at 20+ FPS with sub-50ms latency, buffered into words, grammatically smoothed into fluent sentences, spoken aloud via Text-to-Speech (TTS), and streamed over peer-to-peer WebRTC video to another participant across the globe.

1.1 The Accessibility Challenge

Over 430 million people worldwide experience disabling hearing loss (WHO). Traditional remote communication methods present severe friction:

  • Typing Fatigue: Sign language is often a native primary language with spatial and grammatical structures that differ from spoken language. Typing while on video calls is distracting and slow.
  • Lack of Low-Latency Machine Translation: Cloud-only video AI APIs introduce prohibitive latency (2–5 seconds per sign), rendering natural two-way conversation impossible.
  • Hardware Barrier: Many existing solutions require specialized hardware (e.g., Leap Motion sensors, data gloves), which are costly and inaccessible.

1.2 The Sign Connect AI Solution

Sign Connect AI eliminates all specialized hardware by operating entirely with standard consumer webcams and web browsers. Key technical innovations include:

MediaPipe + 1D-CNN Pipeline

Extracts 21 3D hand landmarks and feeds an axis-aligned 63-element feature vector to a custom 6-layer PyTorch CNN running at >20 FPS.

Decoupled Async Inference

Socket.IO request handlers never block. Frames drop into an atomic per-connection buffer drained by a dedicated background worker thread.

Multi-Mode Recognition

Instant hot-switching between SIBI Alphabets (A-Z), SIBI Numbers (1-9), and 3D heuristic special gestures (I LOVE YOU, STOP, WATER, etc.).

Grammar Smoothing & Autocomplete

Debounced LanguageTool engine, word suggestion trie, and rule-based telegraphic-to-fluent English expansion convert isolated signs to natural sentences.

Full WebRTC P2P Video Mesh

Room-scoped STUN signaling enables zero-latency video calls between participants across NATs and cellular/broadband connections.

Expressive Avatars & Stickers

Client-side 21-point hand skeleton canvas overlays, 2D avatar reactions, and room-wide animated stickers for instant visual feedback.

2.2 Core Architectural Principles

  1. Zero Blocking on the WebSocket Loop: In earlier architectures, executing PyTorch inference directly inside the Socket.IO event handler blocked all other incoming messages (including WebRTC ICE signals and chat messages). Sign Connect AI moves inference entirely into an asynchronous InferenceWorker thread. The socket handler takes a frame, updates the client buffer, and returns immediately.
  2. Stale Frame Dropping: Real-time telecommunication requires immediacy over completeness. If the neural network takes 40ms to execute, queued frames from 80ms ago are discarded. The worker always processes the freshest frame available for each connection.
  3. Reconnection Resilience via Session Tokens: If a user's network experiences a temporary hiccup, the client reconnects with a cryptographic session token. All accumulated sentence state, mode configuration, and autocomplete suggestions are instantly restored without loss.

3. Computer Vision & Deep Learning Subsystem

The core intelligence of Sign Connect AI is housed in recognition_service.py, CNNModel.py, and the supporting landmark processing modules. It transforms raw RGB video pixels into classified sign characters and full-phrase gestures.

3.1 Hand Tracking with Google MediaPipe

The first stage employs Google MediaPipe Hands (v0.10.14) configured with max_num_hands=1, model_complexity=1, and detection/tracking confidence thresholds of 0.6. MediaPipe uses a two-stage detector-tracker pipeline:

  • Palm Detector: Runs across the full frame to locate hand bounding boxes.
  • Hand Landmark Model: Operates on the cropped palm region to predict 21 3D coordinates (x, y, z) with high sub-pixel precision.
MediaPipe 21 Landmark Topology

[0] WRIST • [1-4] THUMB (CMC, MCP, IP, TIP) • [5-8] INDEX (MCP, PIP, DIP, TIP) • [9-12] MIDDLE (MCP, PIP, DIP, TIP) • [13-16] RING (MCP, PIP, DIP, TIP) • [17-20] PINKY (MCP, PIP, DIP, TIP).

3.2 Feature Normalization & Strict Training Parity

A common failure in ML systems is a mismatch between feature preprocessing during training and live inference. In Sign Connect AI, landmark normalization strictly adheres to the translation-invariant formula established during training in handLandMarks.py:

For each coordinate axis among x, y, and z, the minimum value across all 21 landmarks is determined independently: 

x_min = min(x_0, x_1, ..., x_20)
y_min = min(y_0, y_1, ..., y_20)
z_min = min(z_0, z_1, ..., z_20)

The 63-element feature vector is assembled in strict interleaved order matching the training dataset DataFrame serialization: 

features = [
    x_0 - x_min, y_0 - y_min, z_0 - z_min,
    x_1 - x_min, y_1 - y_min, z_1 - z_min,
    ...
    x_20 - x_min, y_20 - y_min, z_20 - z_min
]  # Total size = 21 * 3 = 63 floats

This normalization ensures complete translation invariance: a hand held at the top-left of the screen generates the exact same numeric feature vector as a hand held at the bottom-right.

3.3 Temporal Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Smoothing

Webcam sensors and lighting conditions introduce high-frequency landmark jitter. To prevent inference instability, recognition_service.py maintains a per-connection EMA state vector: 

features_smoothed = alpha * features_current + (1.0 - alpha) * features_previous
# Default smoothing factor: alpha = 0.5

3.4 PyTorch CNN Model Architecture (CNNModel)

The neural network is a deep 1D Convolutional Neural Network defined in CNNModel.py. The 63-element normalized vector is shaped into tensor dimension (batch_size, 63, 1) and passed through progressive feature extraction stages:

Layer Name Kernel Size Stride / Pad Channels / Units Activation / Regularization Output Dimension
Input Tensor — — 63 — (1, 63, 1)
Conv1D Layer 1 3 s=1, p=2 32 BatchNorm1D, ReLU (1, 32, 3)

3.5 Dual-Class Confidence Margin Thresholding

In real-world sign language, gestures such as 'M' vs 'N' or 'A' vs 'S' share subtle topological similarities. Standard softmax argmax frequently causes rapid character flickering when confidence is divided between two classes.

To prevent erroneous output, Sign Connect AI executes a Top-2 Margin Test: 

top2_probs, top2_indices = torch.topk(probabilities, 2)
top1_prob = top2_probs[0].item()
top2_prob = top2_probs[1].item()

margin = 0.06 if mode == "number" else 0.15

if (top1_prob - top2_prob) < margin:
    # Ambiguous prediction: reject frame rather than outputting false character
    return {"character": "—", "confidence": 0, "hand_detected": True}

In addition to trained single-letter CNN weights, recognition_service.py features a 3D geometric classifier that directly decodes complex communicative gestures using Euclidean distance and angle vectors:

3.7 Character Buffering & Temporal State Machine

Once an individual frame is classified, it enters the temporal state machine in sockets/events.py. This state machine prevents double-counting, enforces stability, and handles word building:

  • Sliding Window: Keeps the last 7 predictions with their confidence scores.
  • Score Accumulation: Score(c) is the sum of confidence values for character c. A character must achieve at least 4 stable frames (≥ 240 aggregate score) to be confirmed. Special gestures require only 2 stable frames (≥ 140 score) for instant response.
  • Repeat Sign Prevention: The same sign cannot trigger consecutively unless the hand is removed from the camera view (hand_was_lost = True) or an adaptive cooldown period has elapsed.

4.2 Hardware Stream Management & Camera Off Protection

A recurring bug in web video applications is that disabling the camera in software leaves the webcam physical indicator LED illuminated, causing battery drain and privacy concerns.

Sign Connect AI resolves this via explicit hardware track termination in static/js/communication.js: 

function stopLocalCamera() {
    if (localStream) {
        localStream.getTracks().forEach(track => {
            track.stop(); // Halts hardware capture & releases physical camera lock
        });
        localStream = null;
    }
    localVideo.srcObject = null;
    localCard.classList.remove("camera-active");
    localPlaceholder.style.display = "flex";
}

When the camera is re-enabled, navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia() re-acquires a fresh stream and re-binds video tracks across existing WebRTC peer connections via sender.replaceTrack().

5. Natural Language Processing & Autocomplete Pipeline

Sign language fingerspelling is inherently discrete. To make communication natural and conversational, Sign Connect AI contains an integrated NLP subsystem divided into lexical autocomplete, contextual sentence recommendation, and grammar smoothing.

5.1 Lexical Trie & Prefix Autocomplete (autocomplete.py)

As individual characters are recognized, get_suggestion(current_word) evaluates the active prefix against a prioritized lexicon of essential communicative phrases: 

# Example: User fingerspells 'H' -> 'E' -> 'L'
get_suggestion("HEL") -> "LO"   # Ghost completion displays "LO"
# Pressing [Space] or clicking [Accept Word] instantly completes "HELLO"

5.2 Contextual Sentence Starters

In addition to single-word completions, get_sentence_suggestions() generates interactive pill-shaped suggestion chips based on the initial character or keyword:

Leading Sign Contextual Recommendations Generated
H "HOW ARE YOU", "HAVE A GOOD DAY", "HELLO", "HELP"
I "I AM FINE", "I NEED HELP", "I LOVE YOU", "I AM SORRY"
W "WHAT IS YOUR NAME", "WHERE ARE YOU", "WAIT A MINUTE"
T "THANK YOU SO MUCH", "TAKE CARE", "THAT IS AWESOME"

5.3 Telegraphic Expansion & Debounced Grammar Smoothing (smoothing.py)

Sign language grammar often follows a topic-comment or telegraphic structure (e.g., "I GO STORE" rather than "I am going to the store"). Sign Connect AI handles this with a two-tier engine:

  1. Instant Rule-Based Expansion: Pure Python dictionary lookup executed per sign without latency. Maps shorthand phrases into fluent English:
    • "I GO STORE" → "I am going to the store."
    • "YOU WANT FOOD" → "Do you want some food?"
    • "I PAIN" → "I am in pain."
    • "WATER PLEASE" → "Can I have some water, please?"
  2. Debounced LanguageTool Correction: Full NLP engines (like LanguageTool) spawn a Java process that takes 200–500ms per sentence. In earlier prototypes, executing this on every video frame produced severe lag. Sign Connect AI debounces LanguageTool: it runs only when the user presses Space or clicks Send Sign.

6. Interactive Avatar, Stickers & Audio Services

Sign Connect AI incorporates multimodal visual and auditory channels to enrich communication for both signing and non-signing participants.

6.1 Real-Time Hand Landmark Canvas Overlay

When AI recognition is active, the server returns the 21 normalized landmarks (x, y, z) within the recognition_update socket payload. The client overlays an HTML5 canvas directly atop the local video card, drawing:

  • 21 landmark joints with depth-modulated radius and vibrant neon styling.
  • 20 connecting skeletal bones mapped across the palm and fingers.
  • Color-coded indicators for high (>80%) vs moderate (<80%) confidence.

6.2 Animated Room Stickers

When special communicative gestures are confirmed (e.g., I LOVE YOU, YES, NO, STOP), the server dispatches a sticker_gesture event to all peers in the room. The browser animates a high-resolution contextual visual badge across the video viewport with physics-based entry and exit transitions.

6.3 Text-to-Speech (TTS) Proxy & Multi-Language Audio

To allow non-signing participants to hear what is being signed without looking at the screen, Sign Connect AI includes a speech synthesis module:

  • Web Speech API: Native client-side speech synthesis with automatic fallback.
  • Server-Side TTS Proxy (/api/tts): Proxies multi-lingual audio synthesis (English, Hindi) to prevent browser CORS and referrer blocking.
  • Language Selection: Instant switching between Indian English (en-IN) and Hindi translation (hi-IN).

6.4 Interactive SIBI Sign Language Guide

Integrated directly into the bottom dashboard is an interactive visual reference guide. Users can cycle through all 26 alphabets (A–Z), 9 numbers (1–9), and 8 special gestures, viewing hand shape instructions while practicing in front of their webcam.

10. Automated Testing, Regression Suites & Troubleshooting

10.1 Regression Testing Strategy

To ensure system stability, Sign Connect AI incorporates automated tests guarding against critical failure modes:

  • Feature Ordering Lock: TestFeatureOrdering tests verify that landmark normalization produces identical 63-element feature vectors across random seeds, matching the training pipeline within float32 precision (1e-6).
  • State Preservation: test_reconnection_state_preservation verifies that if a client disconnects and reconnects with a session token within 30 minutes, all in-progress words and sentences are preserved.
  • Sliding Window Stability: Tests ensure that low-confidence frames (<60%) or sporadic one-frame flickers never append incorrect letters.

10.2 Troubleshooting Matrix & Common Pitfalls

Symptom Root Cause Solution
Laptop Camera LED stays ON after clicking "Turn Off" MediaStream tracks were set to null without calling track.stop(). Resolved in communication.js. The function iterates through stream.getTracks() and invokes track.stop().
Remote video does not connect between two devices Direct peer connection blocked by symmetric NAT without STUN/TURN. Verify STUN server (stun:stun.l.google.com:19302) is reachable. Ensure both devices join the exact same room ID.
"Camera permission denied" error in browser Webcam accessed over unencrypted HTTP from a non-localhost IP. Use http://localhost:5000 locally, or launch cloudflared.exe to obtain an HTTPS link for multi-device access.
Audio does not play when Text-to-Speech triggers Browser Autoplay Policy blocks audio prior to user interaction. User must click anywhere on the page or click "Enable Voice" once to grant audio playback authorization.
Gestures not recognized in Alphabet mode Mode separation design prevents letters from colliding with gestures. Switch mode to Special Gestures (or press G) to activate 3D heuristic gesture recognition.
